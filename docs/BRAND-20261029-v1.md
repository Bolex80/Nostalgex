# BolexTV — Brand Assets

**Status:** ratified — **Guide Play chosen by Alex, 29/10/2026** · **Version:** v1 · **Date:** 29/10/2026
**Source of truth:** `brand/build-brand-assets.py` — geometry, palette and every export come from
that one script, so the SVG master and the PNGs cannot drift apart.

Rebuild everything with:

```bash
python3 brand/build-brand-assets.py      # → brand/assets/
```

---

## 1. Reading the mark

The mark has to say two things at once: *this is a Plex library* and *this is a TV guide*.

| Element | What it says |
|---|---|
| Amber rounded tile | the lit screen; the warm amber energy of a media app, in our own hex |
| Play triangle | "it is playing now" — the drop-in-mid-programme behaviour that defines the product |
| Three guide rows, longest first | the EPG: a channel listing, a schedule, programs of unequal length |
| Ink negative space | the dark of a living room; the gaps between programmes |

The single idea carrying the rest: **a programme is already playing inside a schedule.**

---

## 2. The three candidates

| Option | Composition | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **Guide Play** ★ **chosen** | amber tile; ink play triangle left; three ink guide rows right | clearest at launcher size; reads as "play + listing" without explanation |
| Tuner Dial | ink tile; amber ring; cream needle and hub | the most nostalgic reading (a set-top tuner) but says "hardware", not "guide" |
| EPG Grid | ink tile; six amber programme blocks; one cream "now" cell | the most literal guide; weakest silhouette at small size because it has no outer frame |

**Decision: Guide Play**, chosen by Alex on 29/10/2026. It is the only one whose silhouette survives
without the colour doing the work, and the cream cell in EPG Grid competes with the tile for
attention at 64 px.

---

## 3. Palette

Three colours, no more.

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Brand | `#E9A13B` | the tile, the "TV" in the wordmark, the banner rule |
| Neutral | `#12151A` | tile (options 2–3), negative space, banner background |
| Accent | `#F2EAE1` | the "Bolex" wordmark, the dial needle |

`#E9A13B` is deliberately **not** Plex's `#E5A00D`. Plex's mark, wordmark and palette assets are
trademarks and this repository is public; the brief was "Plex-flavoured", not "Plex's logo". No Plex
asset was traced, sampled or embedded.

## 5. Known compromises (stated, not hidden)

1. **At 40 px the three guide rows merge into one mass.** The play triangle still reads, and the
   tile still reads, but the row count is lost. Measured on the contact sheet, not guessed.
   This is acceptable because the Android TV launcher shows a 320×180 banner or a large card —
   40 px is a *phone* surface and BolexTV has no phone build in scope. If one is ever added, ship
   a two-row simplification of the same mark rather than shrinking this one.
2. **The wordmark is set in DejaVu Sans Bold**, resolved from the system. The banner PNG is
   reproducible on this host but not guaranteed pixel-identical elsewhere. If the banner ever needs
   to be built in CI, the text must be converted to paths first.
3. **Circles crop the tile corners** when the mark is used as a round avatar. Nothing meaningful
   lives in the corners, so the crop is clean — verified by sampling all four corners of every
   circular crop (see §6).

## 6. Verification performed

- Rendered with `rsvg-convert` 2.61.3 from the committed SVG masters.
- Contact sheets at 160 / 96 / 64 / 40 px, on both `#12151A` and `#F7F7F7`.
- Corner transparency of the master confirmed: `alpha(2,2) = 0`.
- Corner pixels of all twelve circular crops sampled and compared to the sheet background: all
  clean, i.e. the tile does **not** leak outside the circle. (A vision pass reported that it did;
  the pixel sample disproved it — verify renders mechanically, never from a description.)
- Banner checked at 320×180: wordmark inside the right margin, mark and text vertically centred,
  nothing clipped.
