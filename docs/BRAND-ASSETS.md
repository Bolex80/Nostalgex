# Store badges and other people's marks

Where the third-party badges on nostalgex.app come from, and the rules that come with them. Checked 2026-10-21.

## Apple: "Download on Apple TV"

- **File:** `public/badges/download-on-apple-tv-black.svg`, unaltered.
- **Where it goes:** it is a supporting mark, never the main button. Once per page, 44 px tall, next to the page's main Apple TV button: under the hero buttons where the hero has one (home, Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, Apple TV), under the buttons at the end of a blog post, and in the Apple TV tile on a page whose hero has no Apple TV button (Roku). The buttons themselves are the house `.btn`, and a button to the App Store leads with the TV glyph, not the Apple logo.
- **Source:** Apple's App Store Marketing Tools, `https://toolbox.marketingtools.apple.com/api/v2/badges/download-on-apple-tv/black/en-us`. It is the same file the App Store Marketing Guidelines page shows (`badge-download-on-apple-tv.svg`, 8,699 bytes, its title reads `Download_on_Apple_TV_Badge_US-UK_RGB_blk_092917`).
- **Terms:** the App Store Marketing Guidelines, `https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/`. The ones that matter here:
  - Use only Apple's artwork. Don't modify, angle or animate it. The gray border is part of the black badge.
  - Black is the preferred badge. White is an alternative only when the black one looks heavy and it is the only badge in the layout.
  - At least 40 px tall on screen. Clear space of a quarter of the badge's height on every side.
  - One badge per layout, in a subordinate position to the main message.
  - Credit Apple wherever legal notices go: "Apple, the Apple logo, Apple TV and App Store are trademarks of Apple Inc., registered in the U.S. and other countries."
  - Don't use the standalone Apple logo. It appears only inside the badge.

## Roku: no badge

- Roku publishes no "Available on Roku" or "Get it on Roku" badge for apps.
- **Logo:** the Roku Distribution Agreement (last updated 2026-08-28, section 7.2(a)) lets a developer use the Roku logo "solely to promote your Product's availability on the Platform and only in accordance with the Roku Trademark Guidelines". The guidelines (`https://docs.roku.com/published/trademarkguidelines/en/US`) allow only artwork that Roku provides, unaltered, in Roku's purple. No public download of that artwork was found, so the site uses none. Ask Roku for it before adding one.
- **Text:** the word Roku in plain text is fine, with the notice "Roku is a trademark and/or registered trademark of Roku, Inc. in the United States and other countries." where legal notices go.
- **Publicity:** section 22.1 of the same agreement asks for Roku's written approval (pr@roku.com, five business days ahead) before public communications that reference Roku in connection with the app, apart from the 7.2(a) use above.
