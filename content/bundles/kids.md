This is the bundle for the hour between getting home and dinner, when the TV goes on and nobody wants a negotiation about what to watch.

Fifteen channels across the whole family range. FAMILY HOUR is the mixed channel, the one you put on when there are different ages in the room. ANIMATION and TOON TOWN carry the animated films and the cartoons. FAMILY TV is the live action family shows. TWEEN TV and TEEN DRAMA sit at the older end, for the kids who have decided cartoons are beneath them and would like everyone to know it.

The practical reason a channel beats a menu here is simple: a menu is a shopping trip. Hand a six year old a grid of two hundred posters and you have started a twenty minute conversation about whether they are allowed the one with the scary bit. A channel that is already playing skips all of it. It was on, it is halfway through, that is the end of the discussion.

The other reason is that it only plays what you own. Every channel in this bundle is assembled from your own Plex or Jellyfin library, so the pool is whatever you put on the server and nothing else. There is no catalogue behind the app, no recommendations from somewhere else, nothing that can appear out of nowhere. If a title is not on your shelf, it cannot turn up on a channel.

Nostalgex reads the library, sorts the family titles into these channels, builds a real schedule and starts playing partway through, the way a TV channel does. Channels only appear once there is enough in your library to fill them, which is why a server with no cartoons on it shows fewer channels and that is the right behaviour.

Free, open source under the MIT license, runs on Apple TV with tvOS 18 or later, works with Plex and Jellyfin.
